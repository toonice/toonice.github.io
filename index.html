Featured build

Several — house-share breakup under joint-and-several.

The product portals will not touch

Rightmove sells the room. The fight starts after keys. Several is a rules engine for England from 1 May 2026: one named tenant serves notice on a joint assured periodic tenancy, and the contract dies for everyone. Remaining occupiers need a new grant. A replacement housemate does not slide into the old names.

Occupancy type is the whole product. Joint APT, separate room contracts, lodger. Notice snaps to a rent-due day or the day before. Deposits stay one pot. Guarantors follow the deed, not the friendship. Council tax is classified, not guessed.

Operating model

What I actually know about HMOs.

01

Monk still applies

One valid NTQ on a joint periodic tenancy determines the lot. Wanting to stay is not a right to remain on dead names.

02

Joint and several

The landlord can chase any named tenant — or their guarantor — for the whole rent until the tenancy actually ends.

03

Room lets are the control

Separate contracts in one building. One notice, one room. The mess moves to HMO, council tax and deposit structure.

04

Lodgers are not APTs

Resident-landlord excluded occupiers: one period, weaker security, deposit protection often does not bite the same way.

05

TFA 2019 still bites

Five weeks under £50k annual, six weeks above, holding deposit one week. Excess is a prohibited payment, not a negotiation.

06

Deeds die with the grant

Guarantors cover this agreement. They do not silently guarantee the replacement tenancy. New grant, new deed.
