Rules engine

Several — house-share breakup under joint-and-several.

What it does

You set occupancy, who is leaving, notice or surrender, deposit, guarantors, council tax. It returns a determination: earliest end date, who is still liable for the whole rent, whether stayers need a new grant, and what to do with the pot and the deeds.

Joint APT, separate room contracts, lodger. Dropdowns only offer what that occupancy can actually do. Informal “shares” are not the contract on a joint let.

Scope

England only

Assured periodic tenancies from 1 May 2026. Wales occupation contracts, Scottish PRTs and NI are out on purpose.

Cash collection

Joint exposure — when occupiers miss payments.

What it does

Monte Carlo of 12 months of rent. Defaults are correlated (Gaussian copula), because people in one kitchen miss together. Joint occupancy: remaining names can still be chased for the whole rent, cash-constrained. Room lets: a miss is a hole in that contract only. Deposit recovery capped at the Tenant Fees Act. Guarantors are leaky.

github.com/toonice/joint-exposure

Use

Compare occupancy on the same fabric

Wilton Road joint vs Wilton rooms is the control. Joint looks safer on mean collection. The left tail (VaR95 shortfall) is where rooms lose more, until everyone misses together.

Yield

HMO licence book — does the fifth bed pay?

What it does

Same fabric, beds 2 through 8. Voids, mandatory licensing at five occupiers, additional licensing, amenity capex, bills, insurance, council tax. Then OLS on cash-on-cash yield with HC3 standard errors.

Use

Stop quoting gross yield as 4-bed plus a room

The fifth occupier is usually a mandatory HMO. The book says whether NOI / purchase still rises after the licence and the extra kitchen.
